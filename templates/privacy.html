2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated by your use of the App, and technical data from your device.

Account Data

  • Full name and email address provided at registration.
  • Password (stored as a one-way hash — never in plain text).
  • Profile information you optionally add (display name, profile photo).

Financial Data

  • Expense records you create: amount, date, category, description, and any notes.
  • Custom categories and budget limits you define.
  • Historical transaction data you import or enter manually.

{{ app_name }} does not connect to your bank accounts, payment cards, or any financial institution. All financial data in the App is entered by you manually.

Usage Data

  • Pages visited, features used, and actions taken within the App.
  • Time and duration of sessions.
  • Error logs and crash reports to help us diagnose issues.

Technical Data

  • IP address, browser type and version, operating system.
  • Device identifiers and screen resolution.
  • Referring URLs and search terms that led you to the App.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account, and authenticate your identity on sign-in.
  • Display and process the financial data you enter, including expense summaries, category breakdowns, and trend reports.
  • Send transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset, important notices).
  • Improve the App's features, performance, and user experience based on usage patterns.
  • Diagnose technical problems and respond to support requests.
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing and improving the Service.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS (HTTPS).
  • Hashed storage of passwords using a secure one-way algorithm.
  • Access controls that restrict which personnel can view user data.
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data, and you use the Service at your own risk.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected users without undue delay.

6. Cookies and Tracking

{{ app_name }} uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how you use the Service.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: Required for the App to function — for example, session cookies that keep you logged in.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the App so we can improve it. These are aggregated and non-identifying.
  • Preference cookies: Remember settings such as your selected date format or display currency.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. If you disable essential cookies, parts of the Service may not function correctly.
